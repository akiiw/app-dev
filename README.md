My first repository

**The Hunger Games** is a dystopian science fiction series written by *Suzanne Collins* and adapted into a blockbuster film franchise. Set in the post-apocalyptic nation of **Panem**, the story follows **Katniss Everdeen** after she volunteers to take her younger sister's place in a televised fight to the death.

> *"May the odds be ever in your favor."*
> 
### Main Characters
* **Katniss Everdeen** – The resourceful "Girl on Fire" from District 12 and the reluctant symbol of the rebellion.
* **Peeta Mellark** – The baker's son from District 12 known for his empathy, strength, and loyalty.
* **Haymitch Abernathy** – District 12's only surviving victor who mentors Katniss and Peeta.
* **President Coriolanus Snow** – The ruthless ruler of Panem.

### Movie Installments
1. *The Hunger Games* (2012)
2. *The Hunger Games: Catching Fire* (2013)
3. *The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1* (2014)
4. *The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2* (2015)
5. *The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes* (2023)

### Why It Stands Out
* **High-stakes survival** and smart strategy under extreme pressure
* **Deep themes** of loyalty, media control, and resistance against oppression
* **Distinct world-building** across the Capitol and the 12 districts
